Who this journey is for
  • You feel disconnected from yourself and are ready to come home
  • You’ve explored personal growth and spirituality but long for deeper embodiment
  • You’re tired of endlessly seeking and want to live what you already know
  • You value community and shared transformation
  • You want a simple, grounded daily practice to begin the new year

You do not need to be experienced in meditation. You do not need to have everything figured out. You simply need a willingness to show up.

How much time does it take?

Around 5 minutes each morning with the day’s practice, plus an optional guided meditation of 15 to 30 minutes whenever it suits you. The rest is simply living the practice through your day. The opening and closing live calls are about an hour each, on Zoom.

Do I need the app?

Yes. The app is at the heart of the journey: it’s where the daily practices, the guided meditations, the notifications and the wallpapers all live. Everything you need to get started is sent to you after you join.

What if I miss a day or a live call?

Every live call is recorded and shared in the WhatsApp circle, so you can watch when it suits you. There’s no falling behind, only coming back.

Do I need any experience?

No. You don’t need to have meditated before or hold any particular beliefs. The energy is love and unity, and all are welcome.
